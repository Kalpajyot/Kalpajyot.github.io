01 / ABOUT

I am a PhD scholar at the Centre for Neuroscience, Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore.

My research looks at how the brain makes motor decisions and controls movement. I combine biophysical signal processing and computational neuroscience to study how actions are planned and executed, with a focus on decoding movement intention from neural and physiological signals.
