02 / RESEARCH

Questions I keep returning to

01

Motor decisions

How does the brain weigh options and commit to a movement? Every action begins with a choice, often made in a fraction of a second. I study how the brain gathers evidence, weighs competing options, and decides when it has enough information to act, tracing that process from the first signs of deliberation to the moment of commitment.

02

Motor control

How are intentions transformed into precise, adaptive actions? Once a decision is made, the brain must turn an abstract goal into coordinated muscle activity. I'm interested in the computations that make movement accurate yet flexible, and in how the motor system corrects errors and adapts as conditions change.

03

Intention decoding

Can biophysical signals reveal movement before it begins? Neural and physiological signals carry traces of what we're about to do, even before we move. Using signal processing and computational models, I work on reading out these intentions early and reliably, with applications in brain–computer interfaces and assistive technology.
